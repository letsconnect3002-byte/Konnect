# Jana — The Private Trust Network

Jana is a premium, privacy-first networking and secure messaging application. Designed for professionals, creators, and individuals who value high-integrity connections, Jana replaces traditional social media noise with a curated circle of trust. 

Rather than public directories and unsolicited messages, connections on Jana are established solely through verified, intentional pathways: **direct QR code scans**, **single-use VIP Pass Keys**, or **curated referral introductions**.

---

## 1. The Core Vision: Quiet, High-Trust Networking
Traditional networks often expose your contact details to spam and make it easy for third parties to scrape your profile. Jana changes this equation:
- **Intimacy by Default**: You only connect with people you know, or people who have been introduced to you by a mutual connection.
- **Granular Privacy Controls**: Share your professional profile (social handles, occupation, bio) without giving away your private contact info. Make your phone number private or public with a single tap.
- **Strict Account Separation**: Even on shared devices, your chats are local, encrypted, and isolated strictly per-account. Logging out guarantees complete data hygiene.

---

## 2. The User Journey & Flow

### Phase A: Seamless, Secure Onboarding
1. **Welcome Screen**: A sleek, dark-themed interface invites you to create your account or log in.
2. **EULA Consent Gateway**: To ensure a safe community, a mandatory agreement checkbox is presented on the auth screen. You can tap the interactive legal links to read the Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and End User License Agreement (EULA). All login and sign-up buttons remain disabled until you agree.
3. **Account Creation**: Register securely via your email and set up your password, or use secure third-party login providers.
4. **Two-Sided Digital Identity (Casual vs. Professional)**: 
   - Every user possesses two distinct profile identities within a single account: a **Casual card** for social connections (avatar, bio, Instagram, Spotify) and a **Professional card** for business contacts (company, job role, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, business email, and phone number).
   - You can easily configure and edit both profiles, choosing what information is shared.
   - Toggle your phone number visibility: Keep it **Private** (visible only to you) or make it **Public** (visible to your established connections).

### Phase B: The Connect Hub (Scanning & Inviting)
The **Connect Hub** is the central dashboard for initiating interactions. It features:
- **Your Connection Code**: A unique, secure numeric code representing your digital footprint.
- **Camera Scanner**: An integrated QR scanner to immediately capture another user's profile card in physical proximity. When a card is scanned, a premium glassmorphic overlay lets you know their profile details are being loaded safely.
- **VIP Pass Keys**: Generate single-use, high-trust codes to send via external links to close contacts.
- **Interactive Invite Share**: Send a customized text inviting close peers to download Jana and connect securely.

### Phase C: The Referral Pipeline (Trust-Based Introductions)
If User A wants to connect with User C, but does not have their direct QR code or Pass Key, they can utilize a mutual connection, User B:
1. **Requesting an Introduction**: User A goes to User B’s profile and requests an introduction to User C.
2. **The Intermediary Review**: User B receives a notification: *"User A wants to connect with User C."* User B can review the request, add a personalized recommendation note, and approve it.
3. **The Recipient Approval**: User C receives a secure notification: *"User B referred User A to you,"* accompanied by User B’s custom note. User C can tap **Connect** to instantly connect with User A, or **Ignore** to dismiss the request.

### Phase D: Event Coordination ("Plans")
The **Plans** feature facilitates private, intentional gatherings and offline meetups within your trusted circle:
- **Upcoming & Historical Feed**: View and track all events you have organized, joined, or been invited to, complete with beautiful skeleton loaders and real-time countdown clocks.
- **Plan Customization**: Schedule custom catch-ups, specifying event title, description, time, location type (In-person or Online), meeting links, and category types.
- **Intentional Invites**: Selectively invite contacts from your Casual or Professional network to participate, with automated push reminders sent leading up to the plan.

## 3. High-Trust Messaging Experience

Once a connection is established, both users unlock a private, direct chat room.
- **Real-Time Delivery & Hub Updates**: Clean, instant messaging. When a new message is received while you are browsing the chat lists, the active conversation card dynamically jumps to the top of your feed and highlights itself with an unread badge to draw your focus.
- **Audio Messaging**: Record and send voice notes with native sound feedback (distinct sound cues for sending and receiving).
- **Message Drafts**: If you start typing a response and navigate away, Jana saves a preview draft in your chat hub, making sure you never lose your thoughts.
- **Strict Data Isolation**: When you sign out, the local messages database helper completely unmaps your session files. Logging in as another user will never expose previous chat histories, and returning to your original account safely restores your historical messages.

---

## 4. Interactive & Polite Notifications

Jana respects your focus. The app's notification engine is designed to be highly interactive yet unintrusive:
- **Interactive Banners (Background)**: When you receive a connection request while the app is in the background, you get a rich system notification with quick actions like **Connect**, **Introduce**, or **Ignore** right from your device's lock screen or notification drawer.
- **Polite Banners (Foreground)**: When you are actively using Jana, native system notification popups are suppressed. Instead, a clean, in-app notification banner drops down inside the application, keeping you in your flow.
- **Read-Only Confirmation Alerts**: Once you approve an introduction request or confirm a connection, confirmation notifications appear cleanly without distracting action buttons.

---

## 5. Absolute Safety & Community Moderation

Jana is committed to maintaining a safe, high-trust environment. A suite of easy-to-use moderation and safety tools is integrated natively into the user experience:
- **Content Flagging**: If you receive an inappropriate, abusive, or spam message, you can long-press it directly inside the chat screen and tap the flag/report button. This opens a visual prompt to select the category (Spam, Harassment, Inappropriate, Other) and share details.
- **Delete and Report Connection**: When managing your connections, you can choose to cleanly delete a connection, or select "Delete and Report" to sever the link while reporting safety concerns.
